A design system without motion tokens is a design system that stops at the edge of the static comp. The moment something moves, every developer invents their own timing and the brand fractures into a hundred slightly different bounces.

Our set is small on purpose. Four durations: fast for hover, base for reveals, slow for large surfaces, and a curtain length for full-screen transitions. Three curves: an expo-out for things arriving, an in-out for things travelling, and a soft ease for anything the reader is meant to ignore. One rule sits above all of it: every change eases, nothing snaps.

Four durations, three curves, one rule: every change eases, nothing snaps.

The tokens live in CSS custom properties and are read by GSAP, so a hover underline and a pinned scene share the same vocabulary. Changing the feel of an entire site becomes a two-line edit. That is what a system is for.

The payoff is not that the site moves more. It is that the site moves the same way everywhere, which the reader experiences as confidence. Nothing on the page feels improvised, because nothing was.
