Website optimization is the work of making an existing website faster, easier to use and better at turning visitors into enquiries. We audit the site, fix what is slowing it down or losing people, and measure the before and after. You keep the site you have. It just starts doing its job.

The quiet fear here is that the whole site needs rebuilding. Sometimes it does, and we will say so. More often a handful of problems account for most of the damage: images several times larger than they need to be, scripts that block the page from showing, a form that is painful on a phone.

What we look at

  • Load speed on a real phone on a mobile connection, not only on a desktop
  • Core Web Vitals, the three measurements Google uses for loading, responsiveness and visual stability
  • Images, video and fonts: size, format and how they load
  • Scripts and plugins that are slowing every page for the sake of one feature
  • The path to enquiry: how many taps it takes to call, book or buy
  • Forms: fields nobody needs, errors nobody can read, buttons too small to press
  • Search basics that are missing: titles, descriptions, headings, structured data

Speed

A slow page loses visitors before they see anything. It also costs you in search, because page experience is one of the things Google measures. Most speed problems come from weight, meaning too much being sent to the browser, and from order, meaning the wrong things being sent first. We reduce the first and fix the second.

Conversion

A fast page that confuses people still loses them. So the second half of the work is the path: is it obvious what you sell, is the next step visible without scrolling on a phone, does the form ask for only what you need. Small changes here often matter more than a redesign.

Common questions

What is website optimization?

Website optimization is improving an existing site so it loads faster, is easier to use and converts more visitors into enquiries or sales. It covers speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, forms and the path to contact.

How do I know if my website is slow?

Open it on your phone using mobile data, not wifi. If you are waiting more than a couple of seconds to see the page, so are your visitors. Google's free PageSpeed Insights tool will give you the measurements.

What are Core Web Vitals?

Core Web Vitals are three measurements Google uses to judge page experience: how quickly the main content loads, how quickly the page responds when you tap, and how much the layout shifts while loading.

Should I optimize my site or rebuild it?

Optimize when the problems are specific and fixable, such as heavy images or blocking scripts. Rebuild when the platform or structure fights every fix. Our audit tells you which case you are in.
