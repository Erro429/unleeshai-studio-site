A preloader exists because assets take time, but its real function is dramatic. It is the black frame before the title card. The reader is never more attentive than while waiting for you to begin, and a spinner spends that attention on nothing.

Ours counts from zero to one hundred in the corner while the wordmark rises letter by letter, one letter replaced by a live video slice. Then the whole screen wipes upward and the hero is already moving underneath. The count is honest, it tracks real loading, but the choreography is the point: the site has a rhythm before it has content.

The reader is never more attentive than while waiting for you to begin.

The second visit is different. We remember that the reader has seen the opening and cut it to a beat. A film does not replay its title sequence every time you pause. Neither should a site.

Half a second is not long. It is long enough to tell the reader that everything that follows was composed, not assembled.
