An ambient loop sits behind type for as long as the reader stays on the page, which can be minutes. Every seam in that loop is a small tap on the shoulder. Five taps and the reader has stopped reading and started watching the video for the cut.

We generate loops at five seconds and hand-select the ones whose first and last frames can be blended without a visible jump. The subject has to be moving slowly enough that the blend is not a stutter, and there must be nothing in the frame with a fixed rhythm, no blinking light, no swinging pendulum, that would reveal the period.

The best loop is the one nobody could tell you the length of.

Then we treat the loop like a photograph: one key light, muted saturation, and a scrim so the type in front stays sharp. The loop provides temperature, not information. The best loop is the one nobody could tell you the length of.

Encoding matters as much as content. We ship two sizes, mute the audio track entirely, and preload only the poster frame. A beautiful loop that stalls the page load is a beautiful loop nobody sees.
