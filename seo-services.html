SEO, or search engine optimization, is the work that gets your website shown when someone searches for what you sell. Our SEO service covers the technical foundations, on-page optimization, local search and the content that earns rankings. We quote each piece at a fixed price and we tell you up front that results take months, not weeks.

Most owners who ask about SEO have been burned once: a monthly fee, a report full of charts, and a phone that did not ring any more than before. So we report in the order that matters: impressions, then clicks, then enquiries. Enquiries are the only number that pays a bill.

What the work includes

  • A technical audit: crawl errors, broken links, slow pages, missing or duplicate titles, thin pages, mobile problems
  • On-page optimization: titles, descriptions, headings, internal links and copy matched to what people actually search
  • Structured data so search engines understand your business, services, prices and FAQs
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile, consistent name, address and phone details, local landing pages
  • Keyword research built on buyer intent, not on the biggest number in a tool
  • New pages and articles for the searches you should be winning, through blog writing
  • Reporting from Google Search Console and analytics, in plain language

How long SEO takes

Plan on months. Google's own guidance on hiring an SEO says to expect four months to a year before improvements are in place and the benefit shows. The first month is foundations. Months two and three are content. From month four the compounding starts: pages move from page three to page two to the bottom of page one. The month-by-month version is in how long SEO takes to work.

Two things shorten the wait. A fast, well-built site gives every page a better start. And specific searches move sooner than broad ones: a page about one service in one place will rank long before a page about an entire industry.

Pricing

SEO is quoted at a fixed price for a defined piece of work: an audit, a set of pages to optimize, a local search setup, a month of content. You know the cost and the deliverable before anything starts. Ongoing work is quoted the same way: a defined month of deliverables, written down.

Common questions

What is SEO?

SEO, or search engine optimization, is the practice of improving a website so it appears higher in search results when people look for what the business offers. It covers technical health, page content, local listings and links from other sites.

How long does SEO take to work?

Usually months, not weeks. Google's own guidance says four months to a year. Impressions tend to move first, then clicks, then enquiries.

How much does SEO cost?

We quote SEO at a fixed price for a defined piece of work, such as an audit, a set of pages or a month of content, once we have looked at your site and your market.

Do you do SEO for websites you did not build?

Yes. We start with an audit of the existing site, fix what is holding it back and then build on it. If the site itself is the problem, we will say so.

Is SEO still worth it now that people use AI?

Yes. AI assistants lean on pages that rank well and read clearly, so good SEO helps in both places. We treat AEO and AI SEO as part of the same job.
