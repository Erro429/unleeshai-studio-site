Our blog writing service produces articles that answer the questions your buyers actually type into Google and ask AI assistants. Each piece is planned around a real search, opens with the direct answer and is structured so it can rank and be quoted. It reads like a person talking, not like a brochure.

Most business blogs are a graveyard: six posts from two years ago about company news nobody searched for. The quiet fear is spending money on more of the same. The fix is to stop writing about yourself and start answering questions.

What you get

  • Topic research: the questions your buyers ask, ranked by how likely the asker is to buy
  • A title written as the question, because that is how people search and how they prompt
  • The direct answer in the first two sentences, then the detail
  • Clear subheadings every few paragraphs, so readers and machines can both find their place
  • Internal links to the service or product the article supports
  • A page title, description and structured data for every article
  • Your voice, your facts and no invented statistics

How we choose topics

We start with cost, time and comparison questions, because the people asking them are close to buying. How much does it cost. How long does it take. Which option is right for me. Is it worth it. An article that answers one of those clearly will do more than ten posts of general advice.

Then we look at what already ranks and what the AI assistants say when asked the same question. If the existing answers are vague, there is room. If they are excellent, we pick a narrower question you can own.

Written for search and for AI answers

The structure that helps an article rank is the same structure that makes it quotable: a question as the heading, the answer straight after it, short paragraphs and consistent facts. That is the content half of SEO and of AEO and AI SEO.

You can read our own in the Journal. Entries such as how much a website costs and how long SEO takes follow exactly this pattern.

Pricing

Blog writing is quoted at a fixed price for a set number of articles, with the topics agreed first. You see the list before anything is written.

Common questions

What does a blog writing service include?

Topic research, a title built around a real search, the article itself with clear subheadings, internal links, and the page title, description and structured data that go with it.

How does blog writing help SEO?

Each article is a new page that can rank for a specific question, and it links readers to the service it supports. Over time a set of focused articles brings in searches a small site could never win with service pages alone.

How long should a blog post be?

As long as the question needs. A cost or how-to question usually takes 800 to 1,500 words to answer properly. Padding does not help. Completeness does.

Who chooses the topics?

We propose a list based on the questions your buyers search for and ask AI assistants, and you approve it before anything is written. Nothing is published with invented statistics.
