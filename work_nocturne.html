( The brief )

A fine-dining room with one candle per table needed a site as dark and patient as its service. Typography carries the menu; light carries the mood.

( The answer )

An experimental deployment developed within UNLEESH Labs. This exhibit pushes the boundary of interaction and layout for the sector and sets the benchmark for the builds that follow it.

( The result )

Reservations moved from a third-party widget into the story itself. The seasonal menu is a scroll-driven sequence, and the cellar archive doubles as the sommelier's working list.
