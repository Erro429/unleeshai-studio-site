AEO stands for answer engine optimization. It is the work of getting your business named, quoted or linked when someone asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews a question. You will also hear it called AIO (AI Overview optimization), GEO (generative engine optimization) or simply AI SEO. They describe the same job, and we do all of it.

The quiet fear is simple: a buyer asks an assistant who to hire, and the assistant names three of your competitors. Search engines hand out a list of ten. Answer engines hand out a recommendation of two or three. You want to be in the recommendation.

What the work includes

  • An AI visibility check: we ask the assistants the questions your buyers ask and record who gets named
  • Structured data that states plainly who you are, what you sell, what it costs and where you work
  • Pages rewritten to answer first: the direct answer in the opening lines, the detail after
  • Question-style headings and FAQ sections that match the way people phrase prompts
  • A check that AI crawlers are allowed in, and an llms.txt summary of the site
  • Consistent facts across your site, Google Business Profile, directories and social profiles
  • A plan for mentions you do not control: reviews, forums, local press and industry roundups

How assistants decide who to name

An assistant recommends the business it can describe with confidence. That confidence comes from three places: a site it can read, facts that agree everywhere it looks, and other people vouching for you. Most assistants can also look things up on the live web when they answer, so a page that ranks well and reads clearly has a head start. That is why AEO sits on top of SEO instead of replacing it.

There is no form to submit and no placement to buy. What you can do is remove every reason an assistant might have to skip you. The full walk-through is in how to get your business recommended by ChatGPT, and the terms are untangled in AEO, GEO and AI SEO explained.

How long it takes

Expect weeks to months. Assistants refresh what they know on their own schedule, and mentions on other sites take time to build. The on-site work, which is structure, answers and consistency, can be done quickly. We test the same set of questions on a regular schedule so you can see the change.

Common questions

What is AEO?

AEO is answer engine optimization: improving a website and its wider presence so AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews name or cite the business in their answers.

Is AEO the same as GEO, AIO and AI SEO?

Largely, yes. GEO means generative engine optimization, AIO usually means AI Overview optimization, and AI SEO is the plain version. The work overlaps almost completely.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and nobody honestly can. Assistants choose their own sources. What we can do is make your business easy to read, easy to verify and well vouched for, then measure the change.

Does AEO replace SEO?

No. It sits on top of it. Pages that rank well and read clearly are the ones assistants tend to draw on, so the two are best done together.

How do I check whether AI assistants mention my business?

Ask three assistants the questions your customers would ask before hiring someone like you, and write down who gets named. Repeat it monthly. We run this check at the start of every AEO project.
