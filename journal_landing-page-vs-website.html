Landing page vs website: which one do you need?

A website is the full picture of a business: what it does, who is behind it, the services, the proof and a way to get in touch. A landing page is a single page built around one offer and one action. You need a website when people are checking whether you are real. You need a landing page when you are sending traffic somewhere and want it to do one thing.

The difference is focus. A website answers every question. A landing page asks one. A good website has navigation, several pages and room to explore. A good landing page removes almost all of that on purpose, because every extra link is a way to leave without acting.

A website answers every question. A landing page asks one.

Use a landing page when the visitor arrives from a specific place with a specific intent: an ad, an email, a social post, a QR code on a flyer. Someone who clicked an ad for a free quote should land on a page about that free quote, not on a home page where they have to find it again.

Use a website when the visitor arrives without a script. People who search your business name, follow a referral or find you through a blog post want to look around before they trust you. A single page with one button feels thin to someone still deciding whether you are legitimate.

Most businesses end up needing both, in that order. The website comes first because it is what search engines and AI assistants read to understand who you are. Landing pages come second, one per campaign or offer, and they borrow the trust the main site has already earned.

A sales funnel is what you get when you connect them: a landing page that makes the offer, a form or checkout that captures the action, a thank-you page that sets the next step, and follow-up emails for the people who were not ready. The landing page is the front door of the funnel, not the whole building.

If the budget only stretches to one, ask where your next ten customers will come from. If the answer is search and referrals, build the website. If the answer is ads or outreach you are about to switch on, build the landing page and point everything at it.
