Traffic generation is the work of bringing the right visitors to your website, meaning people who are already looking for what you sell. We combine search, content, AI answers, email and paid campaigns, and we point all of it at pages built to turn a visit into an enquiry.

A website with no visitors is an expensive secret. The quiet fear is the opposite problem: paying for traffic and watching it bounce. So we treat traffic and conversion as one job. There is no point filling a leaky bucket faster.

Where the traffic comes from

  • Search: pages and articles that rank for what your buyers type, through SEO
  • AI answers: being named by ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, through AEO and AI SEO
  • Content: articles that answer buying questions, through blog writing
  • Email: campaigns to the list you already have, and outreach to the people you want to reach
  • Paid campaigns: search and social ads sent to a dedicated landing page
  • Local: your Google Business Profile, maps and directories, for businesses that serve an area

Fast channels and slow channels

Paid ads and email can bring visitors within days, and they stop when you stop. Search and content take months to build, and they keep working after the work is done. A sensible plan uses a fast channel to bring enquiries now and a slow channel to lower the cost of every enquiry later.

Right visitors, not more visitors

Ten visitors who are ready to buy are worth more than a thousand who are browsing. We choose channels and keywords by intent: what the person was trying to do when they found you. Traffic numbers are easy to inflate. Enquiries are not.

Where the traffic lands

Every campaign points at a page built for it. Ad traffic goes to a landing page that repeats the promise in the ad. Search traffic goes to the page that answers the search. When the landing page has follow-up behind it, that is a sales funnel. If the site itself is slow or confusing, we fix that first through website optimization.

Pricing

We quote the work at a fixed price once we know which channels fit your business: a campaign set up, a set of pages, a month of content. Any ad spend is paid by you directly to the platform, and it is separate from our fee.

Common questions

What is traffic generation?

Traffic generation is the work of bringing visitors to a website through channels such as search, content, AI answers, email, social media and paid ads. Done well, it brings people who are already looking for what the business sells.

What is the fastest way to get traffic to a website?

Paid ads and email are the fastest, often within days. They stop when the spend stops. Search and content are slower to start and keep bringing visitors long after the work is done.

How long does it take to get organic traffic?

Usually months. Google's own guidance on SEO says four months to a year. Specific, low-competition searches can move sooner than broad ones.

Why is my website getting traffic but no enquiries?

Usually one of three reasons: the visitors are the wrong people, the page does not match what they were promised, or the next step is hard to find or hard to complete. We check all three before recommending more traffic.
