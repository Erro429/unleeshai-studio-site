Custom website design is a website planned, designed and coded for one business, instead of a template with a new logo dropped in. At UNLEESH every site is hand-coded. A five-page website starts at $499, and most builds go live two to four days after we agree the brief, three on average.

The quiet fear with any new website is paying for something that looks fine and changes nothing. So we start from the job the site has to do: who needs to find it, what they need to believe, and what they should do next. The design follows from that, not the other way round.

What you get

  • A site designed around your offer and your buyer, not a theme chosen from a gallery
  • Hand-written code: no page builder, no plugin stack, nothing to rent month after month
  • Layouts that work on a phone first, then scale up to a desktop
  • Search foundations from the first commit: page titles, descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and clean headings
  • Pages structured so AI assistants can read and quote them, covered in AEO and AI SEO
  • Motion and imagery where they earn their place, and a speed budget that holds on a two-year-old phone
  • The code and the domain are yours when we are done

How the build works

It starts with a short brief: what the business does, who you want to reach and what the site should get them to do. We reply with a fixed quote and a ship date. Day one is structure, copy and the visual direction. Day two is the home page in the browser, then the remaining pages. Day three is you looking at a working site instead of a mock-up, and telling us what to change before it goes live.

Because you see the real thing early, feedback is about the site and not about a slide of the site. That is most of why the timeline is days instead of weeks.

What it costs

A five-page website starts at $499. Past that we do not guess. More pages, online booking, payments, a product catalogue, a members area or a content system all change the work, so we price them once we know what you need. The quote is fixed and in writing, and it does not move unless the scope does. The longer answer is in how much a website costs in 2026.

Who it is for

Businesses whose current site undersells them, new businesses that need to look established from day one, and owners who are tired of a builder site that looks like everyone else's. If you only need one page for a campaign, a landing page is the better fit. If you already have a site you like and it is slow or invisible, look at website optimization or SEO instead.

You can see three live client builds and ten concept sites on the Work page.

Common questions

How much does a custom website cost?

A five-page custom website from UNLEESH starts at $499. Larger sites and sites with booking, payments or a catalogue are quoted at a fixed price once we know what needs building.

How long does it take to build a website?

Two to four days from an agreed brief to launch, and three is the average. The exact ship date is written into your quote before any work starts.

Do you use templates or page builders?

No. Every site is designed for the business and hand-coded. There is no template, no page builder and no plugin stack underneath, which is why the pages load fast and why you own the code.

Will my website show up on Google?

The foundations are built in: titles, descriptions, structured data, a sitemap, fast pages and clear headings. Ranking for competitive searches takes ongoing work, which is what our SEO service covers.

Do I own the website when it is finished?

Yes. The code and the domain are yours. You are not renting the site from us or from a platform.
