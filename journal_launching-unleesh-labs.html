Every studio has a folder of things that never became client work: a shader that did something strange, a product render lit for a client who chose a different direction, a loop made at midnight for no reason. We decided to publish ours.

Labs is that folder with a filter bar. Three streams: web motion design, motion image generation, and high-end commercial image generation. Each entry is dated, categorised and shown at full size in a lightbox. Nothing is retouched for the archive; the point is to show the sketch, not the frame.

The portfolio shows what we finished. Labs shows what we are thinking about.

The portfolio shows what we finished. Labs shows what we are thinking about. Clients read it to see where the studio is heading before they brief us, which turns out to be a better conversation than any capabilities deck.

The archive will grow by a few entries a month. Some will become client work. Most will stay sketches, which is what a sketchbook is for.
