( The premise )

Imagine an energy company that wants the poetry of the panel field, not the datasheet. We rendered the array at night and let geometry speak.

( The approach )

A self-initiated concept, designed and art-directed in the studio with no client behind it. It exists to show how we would approach this sector if the brief landed tomorrow.

( The concept )

In the concept, live generation data feeds a night-mode dashboard that looks like the array itself. Investor and community pages share the same silence, with numbers appearing only when the reader asks.
